About Northstar

Northstar is the Class 9 Study Portal for the Karma and Shraddha divisions.

A shared space for class notes, questions, and live polls.

Portal status: Online · Built for Karma & Shraddha

Help & Support

Login: Use your division, roll number, and valid access code. Ask your teacher for a code if needed.

Class Notes: Search by chapter or choose a subject filter, then select View PDF.

Doubts: Choose Ask a Doubt to post a question, or open a card to read and add replies.

If you still need help, contact your class teacher.
