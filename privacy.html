Privacy Policy for Northstar

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Northstar Class 9 Study Portal collects basic account details when you log in using Google OAuth, including your name, email address, and profile picture. We also collect student roll numbers and custom display names for account verification.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to maintain account security, prevent duplicate multi-device sessions, and display uploader metadata on study notes, Q&A posts, and poll responses.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your authentication data and uploaded study files are securely processed and stored using Supabase backend infrastructure. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to outside parties.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you may contact the portal administrator via the student portal Q&A section.
